Hershey Fonts Attribution
=========================

The single-stroke "Engraving" font bundled with GratisGraph is derived from
the Hershey Fonts, originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at
the U.S. National Bureau of Standards (now NIST).

The Hershey Fonts are in the public domain as a work of the U.S. Government.
The vector data is distributed under the following long-standing terms that
accompany the USENET/usefor distribution of the Hershey data:

  - This distribution of the Hershey Fonts may be used by anyone for any
    purpose, commercial or otherwise, providing:
      1. The following acknowledgements must be distributed with the font
         data:
           - The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey
             while working at the U.S. National Bureau of Standards.
           - The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally
             created by James Hurt (Cognition, Inc.).
      2. The font data in this distribution may be converted into any other
         format EXCEPT that the acknowledgements listed above must be
         included in any product that uses or derives from the data.
      3. This distribution may be compressed, archived, or otherwise altered
         in form, provided the acknowledgements remain intact.

The .jhf encoding used here is the conventional ASCII representation of the
Hershey vector data.
